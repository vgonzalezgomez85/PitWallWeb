// 01

Introduction

PitWall Control is the slot racing championship manager: the administrative and season side. It does not time races (that is handled by PitWall, the timing app); Control maintains everything around the competition — drivers, teams, technical catalogues, scrutineering, credits, treasury and standings — and talks to PitWall to send the heats and bring back the results.

The app's interface is in Spanish; this manual quotes its labels in Spanish where it helps you find them on screen.

  • Runs on desktop (macOS, Windows and Linux) and on Android (tablet or phone). The data lives on the device itself; to share it between several devices, use the Google Drive copy. To scrutineer with several devices at once on race day, see Scrutineering with several devices.
  • The main navigation, on the left, depends on the active championship (chosen at the top, in the red box): Home, Drivers, Teams, Events, «Resum. Verifi.» (the scrutineering summary), Standings, Treasury and Credits. Treasury and Credits only appear if the championship uses them.
  • At the bottom are the general shortcuts: Free scrutineering, Championships, Catalogues, Google Sheets, CSV templates, What's new (the version history) and the light / dark mode switch. Below them, the installed version.
  • If you have no championship yet, the home screen offers Create championship or Only scrutineer cars (free scrutineering).
In one sentence

You set up the championship and its catalogues once, and from there you only register, scrutineer, collect and publish: PitWall Control carries the rules for you.

// 02

Create the championship

Everything hangs off a championship. From Championships you create a new one and define how it works. The championship you choose as active is the one you see throughout the app. A new championship starts blank: no cups ticked and no fees, so you set your own.

Basic details

  • Name and year.
  • Format: Pairs (2 drivers per team), Individual (1 driver) or 24h (endurance teams of up to 6 drivers).
  • Cups / classes: tick the classes that race (e.g. LMP, GT3, GRUPO C) or add a new one. You must tick at least one to save. They are used to build the standings and to filter the catalogues in scrutineering.
  • Axle width: front and rear maximum (mm) per cup. Leave it empty if that cup is not checked.

Rules

  • Credits: turns on the handicap system, with its regularisation cap. When creating the championship you can import the credits from another championship. If you turn it off, all its fields and the Credits section are hidden.
  • Treasury: turns on per-event fees. Enter the Pagat (the total fee, required) and its split: what goes to the coordinating body («Coordinadora») and what goes to the club. Coordinating body + Club must add up to the Pagat, or it won't let you save.
  • Scoring: number of drop scores (worst events that don't count). The points table is edited in Standings.

Motor (scrutineering)

Here you decide where the motors come from in this championship, so scrutineering doesn't ask every time:

  • Draw («Sorteo»): the organisation draws the motors. Scrutineering only records the motor number.
  • Own («Propio»): each team brings its motor. Scrutineering picks it from the catalogue and checks RPM and uMs.
  • Mixed («Mixto»): each scrutineering sheet records whether the motor is from the organisation or the team's own.

With Draw or Mixed, the number range to draw from appears (motor from / to), needed to run the draw. When creating the championship you must pick one of the three.

Manufacturer, branding and status

  • Limit manufacturer: for one-make championships. You choose the allowed brands and, in scrutineering, the brand drop-downs for pinion, crown, wheels and braid only offer those; any other brand already saved shows as an infringement.
  • PDF branding: the championship's own header title and footer motto. Leave them empty to use the default branding.
  • Visible championship (only visible ones appear in the selector) and Finished championship (season closed: standings sort by net points and the season-end bonus is enabled).
The championship's cups are the key piece: almost everything (teams, catalogues, scrutineering, standings) relies on them. Define them before you start registering.
// 03

Catalogues (your library)

The catalogues are the reusable lists of approved parts. You maintain them once and they feed the scrutineering drop-downs. Each tab has a search box and, where it applies, a cup filter.

  • Cars: model, brand, minimum weight, credits, cup(s) and a reference photo (from the camera or the gallery) to compare with the car the team hands in.
  • Brands: short code and name (the ones offered for pinion, crown, wheels and braid).
  • Wheels: size, axle type (DELANTERA / TRASERA / AMBAS — front / rear / both) and cup(s).
  • Gears: pinions and crowns with their teeth, diameter and cup(s). The teeth offered in scrutineering come from here.
  • Motors: name, reference RPM and gauss, and cup(s).
  • Mounts and Tyres: with cup(s).
  • Chassis: no cup; scrutineering always shows them all.
  • Cups (the master list) and Clubs: support lists.

The cup in the catalogue

An entry with no cup does not appear in any scrutineering. In Cars, Wheels, Gears, Motors, Mounts and Tyres you must tick which cups each entry applies to; when scrutineering an LMP team you only see what is ticked LMP. If nothing is ticked for a cup, the drop-down is empty (and says so). Cups are compared ignoring case, spaces, hyphens and dots: LMP-2, LMP 2 and LMP2 are the same.

The new / edit dialog has the cup selector, and the list shows each entry's Copas: … underneath. You can fill the catalogues by hand, import them from CSV (see CSV templates), in Excel (below) or sync them with Google Sheets (see Google Sheets and Drive).

The whole catalogue in Excel

At the top right of Catalogues, the table-icon button opens two actions that work on all catalogues at once:

  • Export all to Excel: an .xlsx with one tab per catalogue — Coches, Marcas, Llantas, Engranajes, Motores, Bancadas, Chasis, Neumaticos, Copas, Clubs.
  • Import edited Excel: you hand the sheet back and the app applies the changes.

The first column of each tab is the ID: row with ID → that entry is updated; row without ID → a new one is created; row you delete from the sheet → stays in the app (the import never deletes). The Copas column is text separated by semicolons (e.g. GT;GT2). Before applying, the app asks for confirmation and afterwards summarises how many entries were created and updated.

Don't delete the IDs (the entry would be created again as new) and don't rename the tabs: the importer looks them up by name and warns you if it doesn't recognise one.
// 04

Drivers

A driver is a single master record (name, phone, email) who can race in several championships. In each championship they have their own profile:

  • Class (PLATINO · ORO · PLATA · BRONCE), the ladder used by the season-end bonus and promotion between seasons.
  • Credits, initial and current (if the championship uses credits).
  • Coordinating body: if every driver in a team belongs to the coordinating body, the team pays no fee.

From Drivers you add, edit and search. The Import button brings many at once from CSV, Excel or Google Sheets, and the previous score: last season's points of the drivers already registered, used to order the heats of the first event of a new championship. As soon as there are results of its own, the real standings take over.

// 05

Teams and entries

A team groups the drivers who share a car and races in a specific cup. The number of drivers depends on the format:

  • Individual: 1 driver. The screen is called Entries («Inscripciones») and doesn't ask for a team name: the driver's name is used.
  • Pairs: 2 drivers.
  • 24h: 1 to 6 drivers; you add and remove seats in the editor.
The team's cup rules scrutineering: it decides which cars, motors, gears, wheels, tyres and mounts are offered. It can be changed for a single event only (when registering or from the scrutineering sheet itself) without touching the rest of the championship.

You can create them by hand or import them from CSV (the endurance template accepts Piloto 1…5).

// 06

Events and heats

An event is each date on the calendar (name, venue, date, order and status: scheduled, in progress or finished). Opening it shows its heats and, on the left, the event options: entries, results, scrutineering, scrutineering summary, edit heats, motor draw, event treasury, heats and scrutineering PDFs, and the exchange with PitWall.

  1. Create the event and order it in the calendar.
  2. Register the teams (or drivers) who race.
  3. Generate the heats with their lane assignment.
  4. On race day, scrutineer, collect fees and adjust the heats if needed.

Entries

Event entries shows who races, with their cup and preferred day. You can add by hand (person icon with «+», top), import from CSV/Excel or Google Sheets and withdraw someone who doesn't race (removes them from the entries and from their heat). The cup is stored as a snapshot for that event: if the team's cup changes later, the event keeps the one they raced with.

Generate heats

The wizard splits the entries into heats per day:

  • Track lanes and minutes per lane (works out each heat's real duration and spaces the suggested times).
  • Number of heats: as many as full lanes can be filled, with the rest spread among them; the day's last heat is never left half empty.
  • Names and times of each heat.
  • Assign lanes automatically and assign marshals (individual championships): by points, highest first, in lanes 1…N and, if there are more, rests D1, D2…; marshals are shared out among the same day's heats.
  • Preview before creating, and the option to replace existing heats.

The heat, on race day

The heat detail shows the grid: each lane with its driver, the free lanes («Libre») and, at the end, those still without a lane. Tap a driver (or «Change lane» in their menu) and a grid of all lanes appears: tap a free one to move them there, or a taken one to swap them. You can also renumber lanes by points, choose the marshals, remove someone from the heat and see a warning if lanes are repeated. In Edit heats you move drivers between heats (they arrive without a lane, to be assigned there).

// 07

Scrutineering

Scrutineering is the heart of race day: for each team you check that its equipment meets the rules before approving it. You enter from the event (Scrutineering), choose the heat and the team. Each heat shows how many it has approved.

The scrutineering sheet

At the top, the team name and its cup (tap it to change it for this event only). Below, a summary of what fails and a bar with the completed blocks and those still missing. If the championship manages a treasury, the Treasury block lets you collect the fee right there, with the same form as the event treasury.

Each block sits in a box with its status: amber «Pending» if something is missing, green «Done» when complete and red «Check» if something breaks the rules. The blocks are:

  • Car: only models of its cup, with the catalogue's reference photo below for comparison.
  • Body: weight (with the car's minimum) and appearance — «Bien» (fine) or «Faltan piezas» (missing parts), with a note for which ones.
  • Motor: depending on the championship's motor type. Organisation: the number (with a Draw button). Own: the catalogue motor, with RPM and uMs checked live against its reference (✓/✗; both are maximums). Plus motor height to track: compliant / non-compliant.
  • Axle width front and rear, against its cup's maximum (only counts if the cup has one).
  • Front and rear wheel: brand and size, filtered by cup and axle.
  • Pinion and crown: brand, teeth (those in its cup's gear catalogue), diameter and material — pinion PL / ERG / LAT / ACR / ALU; crown PL / MET.
  • Mount, chassis, whole-car weight and others (tyre, braid, suspension).
  • Notes and photos (up to 4), optional.

Infringements and approval

The app flags in red whatever breaks the rules: weight under the minimum, axle over the maximum, RPM or uMs above the reference, motor touching the track, brand not allowed (with limit manufacturer), missing parts… The sheet autosaves as a draft with every change, so you can have several open at once. When everything complies, you approve it.

The drop-downs learn. Car, motor, brands, teeth, materials, wheels, mount, chassis, tyre and braid list first what has been picked most often in saved scrutineering; anything never used stays behind, in its usual order.

Grid summary

«Resum. Verifi.» (in the side bar, in the event or via the grid button in Scrutineering) shows all of an event's scrutineering in a spreadsheet-like table, read only: one row per entry with status, cup, car, weights, motor, axles, gearing, wheels, mount, chassis, tyre, body and notes. Whatever breaks the rules is shown in red (hover to see why). It has search and filters (with infringement, not scrutineered, draft) and exports to PDF and Excel.

// 08

Scrutineering with several devices

When there are several scrutineers, each one can work on their own device (Mac, Windows or Android) on the same event at the same time. Each Control saves on its own device and they pass changes to each other over Wi‑Fi. No internet needed: a router with no connection or a phone's hotspot will do.

Before the event

  • Everyone starts from the same copy of the data: championship, event, heats and teams. The easiest way is to restore the same Google Drive copy on every device.
  • Everyone on the same version of PitWall Control (otherwise the sync warns and doesn't run).
  • Automatic time turned on on every device.

Connecting

In the event, open «Verificar entre varios» (from the event menu or with the sync icon in Scrutineering). On each device:

  1. Give it a different name: «Mesa 1», «Tablet boxes»…
  2. Type the event key («Clave del evento»), the same on all of them.
  3. Turn on «Conectar con otros Controls» (connect with other Controls).

They find each other on the network and appear under «Controls en la red», with the time of the last sync and what arrived. If one doesn't show up (some routers don't allow it), add it with the link button by typing the address shown under «Visible como» on its screen. With «Sincronizar automáticamente» changes are passed every 20 seconds; you can also press «Sincronizar ahora» (sync now). The icon in Scrutineering shows how many Controls are connected.

What is shared

  • The scrutineering sheets, with their photos, and deletions.
  • Each team's cup in the event.
  • Treasury payments, including wildcard and «Coord. total».

Changes travel from one Control to another even if those two haven't synced directly. Credits are never deducted twice: each Control works out its own when it receives an approved scrutineering sheet.

If two people touch the same scrutineering sheet, the latest change wins. If you have a sheet open and another Control changes it, it updates on screen; if you were halfway through editing, it warns you so you can choose «Seguir con lo mío» (keep mine) or see the other one. The easiest way is to split the teams between you.

The app must be open on every device (on Android, if it goes to the background it stops sharing). Free scrutineering isn't synced. The first time, Windows asks for firewall permission and macOS for local network access: accept them.

// 09

Standings

The standings are worked out from the event results and the championship rules:

  • Points table by position, specific to each championship. Edit it from the Points table button: how many positions score and how many points each one gets (or restore the defaults).
  • Drop scores: worst results that don't count.
  • Overall and one tab per cup. The Cup column shows the cup the driver raced in.

You can also fill them in by hand or import them, and export them to PDF.

// 10

Treasury

If the championship uses a treasury, each team pays a fee per event (the Pagat), split between the coordinating body and the club. The Treasury screen shows the championship balance, what was collected at each event and the extra movements (sponsorship, equipment costs… with the «Movimiento» button).

  • Split («Reparto»): change the Pagat and how much goes to each side (with each side's %). You can apply it to payments already collected too: what each team paid is split again, without changing the amount collected.

Collecting at an event

In Event treasury (or straight from each team's scrutineering sheet) you collect with:

  • Pagat / Coordinadora / Club. If you only type the Pagat, the split is filled with the championship's proportion; if it doesn't add up, a warning appears with a «Repartir» (split) button.
  • Quick payment: the full fee in one tap; «Coord. + piloto» (one driver belongs to the coordinating body: pays half); «Coord. total» (all of them do: no fee); «Limpiar» (clear).
  • Wildcard (gift icon): a guest, pays nothing. Those who don't pay aren't counted as pending: the counter shows «X / Y paid · N don't pay».
  • A search box fixed at the top to quickly find who is about to pay.

It saves when you leave each field. The event treasury and the scrutineering sheet always show the same.

// 11

Credits and season close

The credits system is the championship's handicap: each driver starts with some credits that go up or down with results. In Credits you see the balance and the movements (each with its description), can adjust by hand, export to PDF or CSV and manage the season close.

Season-end bonus

At the end of the season a bonus is applied according to the driver's class and the number of races they did (configurable bands). The movement is described (e.g. "bonus for 6 races").

Class review

Before finishing, Class review lets you decide driver by driver whether they move up, move down or stay on the BRONCE→PLATA→ORO→PLATINO ladder. That class is used for the bonus and as the starting class when importing into the next championship.

Adjust the classes in Review first and then apply the season-end bonus: that way both use the same final snapshot.
// 12

Motor draw

If the championship's motor type is Draw or Mixed and it has its number range set, Control draws a number within the range for each team, leaving out those already assigned in the event. From Motor draw (in the event) you draw team by team or «Sortear restantes» (draw the rest) in one go, and the Numbers tab shows who has which. You can also draw from the scrutineering sheet itself.

// 13

Free scrutineering

To scrutineer cars without a championship or event: a one-off race, a club check or any other meeting. It opens from Free scrutineering («Verificación libre») in the side bar (or from home, even if you have no championships).

  • Each session has a name, venue and date, and its own rules: cups, axle width per cup, limit manufacturer and motor type (Draw / Own / Mixed, with its range). A new session starts blank, just like a championship.
  • Participants: add them one by one (driver with autocomplete, optional second driver and team, and cup) or import them from a CSV / Excel file or from Google Sheets. The importer recognises the columns by itself, has a default cup for rows without one and a preview to untick rows. Long-press a participant to remove them.
  • Scrutineering is the usual one: autosave, photos, draw, validation per cup.
  • Export: to PDF or to Excel with photos.

Sessions don't use credits or treasury, aren't sent to PitWall Manager and don't show up among the championships.

Excel with photos

The .xlsx has one row per participant with the grid-summary columns (rule breaches in red and a column with the reasons) and, at the end of the row, the car's catalogue photo («Foto catálogo») followed by all the scrutineering photos, as thumbnails inside the cells. It displays correctly in Excel, LibreOffice and on mobile (iPhone, iPad, Android).

// 14

Google Sheets and Drive

Control can work with your Google sheets and keep copies in Google Drive. First, in Google Sheets, paste your Google Cloud project's credentials (the «Cómo conseguirlas» button guides you) and connect your account. Control only asks to read your sheets and to create or modify its own backup file; it doesn't touch any file of yours.

  • Catalogues: link each catalogue to its tab. Update from Drive brings the data in and Upload to the Sheet sends yours (with a review before deleting). If the sheet has an ID column, rows are matched by it instead of by name.
  • Car photos: a FOTO column with the Drive link of the image. Updating downloads the photo; uploading sends to Drive the photos that only exist in the app and writes their link.
  • Drivers, teams and entries can also be imported from a sheet.
  • Shared copy in Drive: upload or download the database (with the scrutineering photos) to work on several devices, Android included.
// 15

CSV templates

From CSV templates you download ready-to-fill templates to import: drivers, teams (pairs and endurance, with Piloto 1…5), event entries, free scrutineering participants and each catalogue. Fill in the file, import it from its section and the app recognises the columns.

To correct in bulk what you already have in the catalogues, the easy route is The whole catalogue in Excel.

// 16

Export (PDF and Excel)

  • PDF: heats, scrutineering of an event or session, grid summary, standings and credits. When exporting you choose the document language (Spanish, English, Italian or French) and the championship's branding is applied to header and footer. Each PDF fits on a single page (A4 → A3 if needed) for easy printing.
  • Excel: the full catalogue, the grid summary and free scrutineering with photos.
  • CSV: credits.
On the computer a "Save as" dialog opens; on Android, the system share sheet (save to files, send via WhatsApp, email…).
// 17

Send to PitWall / bring back results

PitWall Control talks to the PitWall timing app so you don't type things twice: it sends the prepared event and then brings back the results. Both apps must be on the same network (LAN/WiFi).

Choosing the PitWall

The three exchanges share one selector: for a few seconds you see «Buscando…» (searching) and the PitWalls on the network appear (via Bonjour/mDNS and by probing the local network directly, so it works even if the router blocks announcements). You can press «Buscar de nuevo» (search again) or type the IP:port by hand. It works on macOS, Windows and Android; on Linux automatic discovery isn't available and you have to type the address.

Send the batch (outbound)

Each heat becomes a batch with each team in its lane and the rests (D1…Dk) placed and rotating. Two ways:

  • Export batch (JSON): a file you upload in PitWall (Races → Import batch). When exporting it asks whether the race has a pole.
  • Send to PitWall (over the network): choose the PitWall and enter the pairing PIN shown on PitWall's Import batch screen.
With The race has pole switched on, Control doesn't send the lane order: PitWall creates the race with the pole session and the grid is decided after running it.

Bring back the results (inbound)

When it's over, press Bring back results from PitWall in the event: choose the PitWall and the race, check in the preview how the teams were matched by name, and apply. Each team's position in the race's final standings is stored; Control awards the points with its own table. You can also import the results from a timing file in the event results.

Send the scrutineering

Send scrutineering to PitWall sends the data of what was checked (weights, motor, pinion, crown, wheels, approved, notes…), without the photos, so it can be seen inside the race. Same route: PitWall, PIN and the matching race, which Control remembers for later resends.

In PitWall, scrutineering is read only, and each new send fully replaces the previous one. It requires Ecosystem connection to be allowed in PitWall.

Glossary

  • Championship: the container for the season (format, cups, rules, motor type, modules).
  • Cup / class: the category a team races in. It filters the catalogues and groups the standings.
  • Format: Individual (1 driver), Pairs (2) or 24h (up to 6).
  • Catalogue: library of parts (cars, brands, wheels, gears, motors, mounts, chassis, tyres). An entry with no cup doesn't appear in scrutineering.
  • Motor type: Draw (from the organisation), Own (the team's) or Mixed (chosen on each sheet).
  • Limit manufacturer: only the chosen brands are allowed for pinion, crown, wheels and braid.
  • Event: each date on the calendar; it holds entries and heats.
  • Heat: a run of the event with its lane grid; D1, D2… are rests.
  • Marshals («pisters»): the drivers from another heat who act as track marshals.
  • Scrutineering: checking a team's equipment before approving it, block by block.
  • «Resum. Verifi.»: all of an event's scrutineering in a table, with rule breaches in red.
  • Free scrutineering: a scrutineering session with no championship or event, with its own rules.
  • «Verificar entre varios» (scrutineering with several devices): several Controls scrutineering the same event at once, synced over Wi‑Fi (no internet).
  • Reference photo: the car's image in the catalogue, to compare with the one the team hands in.
  • Pagat: the total fee per event; split between the coordinating body and the club.
  • Wildcard / «Coord. total»: teams that don't pay at that event (guest / all from the coordinating body).
  • Credits: handicap system; they go up or down with results.
  • Season-end bonus: credits added at the end according to class and number of races.
  • Class ladder: BRONCE · PLATA · ORO · PLATINO.
  • Drop score: worst result that doesn't count towards the standings.
  • Previous score: last season's points, used to order the heats of the first event.
  • Batch: the packaged event (pitwall.tanda/v1) sent to PitWall.
  • Pairing PIN: the code PitWall shows and Control asks for to send over the network.
  • Branding: the championship's title and motto on the PDFs.
