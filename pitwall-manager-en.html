PitWall Manager

Manager is installed on the computer next to your track. It connects to your timing system, counts every car’s laps and shows the race in big numbers so the whole room can see it.

Manager is installed on a computer, not on a phone. Open this page on the track computer to download it.
Are you a driver? What you want is PitWall Lap, the phone app.

What it does

Counts laps by itself

Records every lap and every time. If a lap is missed, you add or remove it without stopping the race.

Easy to read from afar

Big numbers and clear colours, made to be projected on a wall or shown on a TV.

Long races

Driver changes, breaks and lane changes for endurance races, such as a 24-hour race.

Drivers follow it on their phones

With PitWall Lap, each driver hears their times out loud. The results go to PitWall Control to score the championship.

What you need

  • 1
    A computer next to the track. Windows, Mac or Linux.
  • 2
    Your timing system. A DS-300, connected with its cable; a BART, via Bluetooth; or a TicTac interface, via USB.
  • 3
    WiFi at the club (only if drivers will use Lap on their phones). No internet needed.
