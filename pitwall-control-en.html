PitWall Control

Control handles everything that happens off the track: drivers and teams, entries, car scrutineering, points for every event, standings and fees. You just enter the data; it does the sums.

Download for Mac

What it does

Standings always up to date

Overall and by cup, with the points from every event. It updates itself when the race day is over.

Drivers and teams

Every driver’s and team’s profile, with their class, in one place.

Car scrutineering

Record each car’s technical check. Several people can scrutineer at once on race day.

Clear accounts

Championship fees, entries and expenses, with the balance always in view.

Works with Manager

Send the heats to PitWall Manager and bring the results back without copying anything by hand.

Share the results

Export the standings to PDF or Excel, or publish them on Google Sheets for everyone to see.

What you need

  • 1
    A computer or a tablet. Windows, Mac with Apple chip, Linux or Android.
  • 2
    PitWall Manager at the track (optional), to bring the results back automatically.
