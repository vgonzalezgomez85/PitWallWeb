PitWall Lap

You can’t look at a screen while you race. Lap tells you out loud every lap, your position and the time left. Afterwards, the race is saved on your phone.

Lap is a phone app. The easiest way is to open pitwall.es on your phone and download it there.

Download for Android

Version 1.22.7. When you open the file, Android may ask whether to allow apps from outside Google Play: say yes. After that, the app updates itself.

What it does

Reads your times out loud

Every lap, your position, the gap to the car ahead and the last minute. You choose what you want to hear.

Finds the race by itself

With your phone on the club WiFi, Lap finds the race in a few seconds. It works with PitWall Manager and with TicTac timers.

Go over the race afterwards

The standings and all your laps are saved on your phone, even with no connection.

Log your practice

Save your practice runs with the car’s setup and compare them to find the best one.

Calendar with reminders

Save your upcoming races and your phone reminds you before each one.

In your language

Spanish, Catalan, Basque, English, Italian, French, Portuguese and Dutch, voice included.

What you need

  • 1
    An Android phone.
  • 2
    Being on the club WiFi (the same one as the track computer). No internet needed.
  • 3
    Your club timing with PitWall Manager or a TicTac timer. The calendar and practice also work at home.
